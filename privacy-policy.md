## 1. Who We Are

Sprout is an iOS app for plant care tracking, AI-assisted plant diagnosis, and feng shui plant guidance. It is developed and operated by Chum Man Lee, an individual developer based in Hong Kong.

For data protection enquiries, contact us at **sprout.app.support@gmail.com**. We aim to respond within **30 days**.

---

## 2. What Data We Collect and Why

We collect the minimum data necessary to operate the app. The table below is a complete inventory.

| Data | Why we collect it | Lawful basis (UK GDPR) | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Apple anonymous user ID** (opaque string from Sign in with Apple) | To link your subscription across app reinstalls and devices | Performance of contract | RevenueCat (see §4) |
| **Plant photos** you submit for AI diagnosis | To provide the plant health diagnosis feature | Consent (you initiate each submission) | Firebase Vertex AI (see §4); deleted from servers after processing |
| **Anonymous usage events** (e.g. "plant added", "diagnosis run", "paywall shown") — no personal identifiers are attached | To understand how features are used and improve the app | Legitimate interests | Firebase Analytics (see §4) |
| **Crash reports and device diagnostics** (device model, OS version, app version, anonymised stack trace) | To detect and fix bugs | Legitimate interests | Firebase Crashlytics (see §4) |
| **Subscription status** (active/inactive, subscription period) | To unlock Pro features you have paid for | Performance of contract | RevenueCat (see §4) |
| **Plant data you enter** (plant names, care notes, watering records, garden photos) | Core app functionality | n/a — stays on your device | **On-device only. Never uploaded.** |

**We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, health data, or any other personal information.**  
Sign in with Apple shares only an opaque anonymous identifier with us — your Apple ID email and real name are never transmitted to Sprout.

---

## 3. Push Notifications

Care reminders are **local notifications** generated entirely on your device by the iOS scheduler. No data leaves your device for notification delivery. We do not operate a push notification server.

We use the following sub-processors, each governed by their own privacy policy. By using Sprout you acknowledge that your data may be processed by these services.

| Processor | Purpose | Data sent | Privacy Policy |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Google Firebase** (Firebase Analytics, Firebase App Check, Firebase Crashlytics) | Usage analytics, API security, crash reporting | Anonymous events, device identifiers, crash traces | [firebase.google.com/support/privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy) |

We do not sell your data to any third party. We do not use your data for advertising or profiling.

## 5. Data Retention

| Data | Retention period |
|---|---|
| Apple anonymous user ID / subscription records | Retained by RevenueCat for as long as your account exists; delete by contacting us |
| Firebase Analytics data | Aggregated; automatically deleted after 14 months per Google's default retention |
| Firebase Crashlytics crash reports | 90 days per Google's default retention |
| Plant photos submitted for diagnosis | Deleted from Google servers after processing (not persistently stored) |
| On-device plant data | Retained until you delete the app or the data within it |

## 6. Your Rights

Regardless of where you are located, you may contact us at **sprout.app.support@gmail.com** to:

- **Access** a copy of any personal data we hold about you
- **Correct** inaccurate data
- **Delete** your data ("right to be forgotten")
- **Withdraw consent** at any time (this does not affect lawfulness of prior processing)
- **Object** to processing based on legitimate interests

Because the only personal data linked to you is the Apple anonymous user ID and subscription status (both held by RevenueCat), deletion can be fulfilled by: (1) contacting us at the email above, (2) using RevenueCat's data deletion flow, or (3) deleting the app (which removes all on-device data).

### UK and EU residents — additional rights

If you are located in the United Kingdom or European Union, you have additional rights under the UK GDPR / EU GDPR:

- **Portability** — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
- **Restriction** — ask us to pause processing while a dispute is resolved
- **Object to automated decision-making** — we do not use automated individual decision-making

**UK residents** may lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): [ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint)  
**EU residents** may lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority.

### Hong Kong residents

You may exercise your rights under the **Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (Cap. 486)** (PDPO) by contacting us directly or by contacting the Office of the Privacy Commissioner for Personal Data (PCPD): [pcpd.org.hk](https://www.pcpd.org.hk)

### Taiwan residents

You may exercise your rights under the **Personal Data Protection Act** (個人資料保護法) by contacting us directly.

## 7. Children

Sprout is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the app, please contact us at sprout.app.support@gmail.com and we will delete it promptly.

This app is rated 4+ and contains no content that would attract use by children under 13.

## 8. Data Security

We rely on industry-standard security measures provided by our third-party processors (Google Firebase and RevenueCat), including TLS encryption in transit and AES encryption at rest. On-device data is protected by iOS device encryption. However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
